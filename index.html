Regular expression to ε-NFA

Thompson's construction. Use | for union, * zero or more, + one or more, ? optional, and ε or ∅ for the empty string and empty language. Put \ before a symbol that looks like an operator.

Automaton to regular expression

State elimination on the automaton currently in the editor. It works for DFAs, NFAs and ε-NFAs. The result is checked against the original.
